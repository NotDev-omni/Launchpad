Why this is the hard part 🎯

⏱️

Four screens, not forty

Most launchpads ask for a wallet, a treasury, a Discord and an audit before you can type a name. Everything here has a sane default.

🔒

Rails you can't disable

Mint authority revoked, LP locked, metadata frozen — enforced by the program, not by policy. Buyers verify it on-chain, they don't trust a badge.

💰

Free to launch

Gas is sponsored and recouped from mint fees. An empty wallet is not a blocker, which is the single biggest drop-off in creator onboarding.
