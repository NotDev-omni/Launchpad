Build notes

What this layout borrows, and what it deliberately doesn't

The shape is theirs

Measured off the live site: 52px icon rail, a persistent ranking sidebar with 44px rows, hero with an overlaid stats strip, and sections as title + one grey subtitle + carousel.

The skin is ours

Their real UI is dark, dense and terminal-like. We keep the information architecture and drop our rounded, warm, light-and-dark language on top.

Two things not copied

Cards clipped mid-scroll read as broken more often than as scrollable, and five grid densities is three too many. We ship three.

Banners are composited

The collection-metadata endpoint rate-limited on all 7 collections even with 60s backoff, so real banners need an API key. These are built from each collection's own item art.

The data is real

Every floor, ask, mint, seller and rank came from keyless public calls. live asks across collections, snapshot .
